## 📖 Workshop Synopsis

The **AI in Video Resume** workshop introduces students to the practical use of **Generative Artificial Intelligence** in creating professional, creative, and engaging video resumes.

Participants will learn how to identify their strengths, shape a personal brand, develop a concise script, plan a storyboard, generate consistent visuals, and assemble a complete video. The workshop combines guided instruction with hands-on activities using ChatGPT, Gemini, Qwen, Google Flow, Dola, Canva, and CapCut.

Working individually or in teams, participants will transform their academic background, technical skills, achievements, and career aspirations into a focused video resume for internship, employment, scholarship, competition, or professional portfolio purposes.

## 🎯 Learning Outcomes

By the end of the workshop, participants will be able to:

1. Explain the role of Generative AI in professional content and video production.
2. Develop a clear personal brand based on strengths, skills, achievements, and career aspirations.
3. Write effective prompts and a concise script for a two-minute video resume.
4. Design a master storyboard and divide it into manageable video segments.
5. Produce and combine AI-assisted scenes into a polished final video.
6. Evaluate a video resume based on content, creativity, visual quality, AI use, and professionalism.

## 🛠️ AI and Multimedia Applications

| Purpose | Recommended Applications |

## 📦 Final Deliverables

Each participant or team must prepare:

- A concise video resume script
- A complete two-minute master storyboard
- Individual ten-second video segments
- A final two-minute AI-assisted video resume
- A final submission for the video resume competition

## ✅ Final Video Checklist

Before submission, confirm that the video:

- communicates a clear and memorable personal brand;
- highlights relevant skills, achievements, and career aspirations;
- follows a logical story from opening to closing;
- maintains consistent characters, clothing, setting, and visual style;
- uses clear speech, readable captions, suitable music, and balanced audio;
- is approximately two minutes long;
- contains accurate, appropriate, and original content;
- acknowledges the use of Generative AI where required; and
- is exported in the required format and submitted before the deadline.

## 🎒 What Participants Should Prepare

- A laptop or suitable mobile device
- Earphones or headphones
- Stable internet access
- Active accounts for the selected AI applications
- Academic, project, achievement, and skills information
- Suitable personal photographs or visual references, if needed
- A folder for organising scripts, prompts, images, audio, and video clips

## 🤖 Responsible Use of Generative AI

- Protect personal and confidential information.
- Do not upload another person’s image, voice, or work without permission.
- Check all AI-generated facts, names, spelling, captions, and visual details.
- Avoid misleading claims about qualifications, skills, or achievements.
- Use copyright-safe images, music, fonts, and other media.
- Treat AI as a creative assistant; participants remain responsible for the final work.
